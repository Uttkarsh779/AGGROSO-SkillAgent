# Refund Policy

## Overview
Our refund policy governs how customers can request and receive refunds for purchases made on our platform.

## Eligibility
- Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the original purchase date.
- Digital products that have been fully downloaded or accessed are eligible for refund review but not automatically refunded.
- Physical products must be in original, unused condition to qualify for a full refund.

## Automatic Refund Cases
The following situations qualify for automatic, no-questions-asked refunds:
- Duplicate charges (customer was charged more than once for the same order)
- Service was never delivered or activated within 72 hours of payment
- Technical error confirmed by our engineering team prevented access to the product

## Payment Deducted But Order Not Created
If a customer reports that payment was deducted but their order was not created:
1. Verify the payment in the billing system
2. Check if the order exists in any pending or failed state
3. If no order exists and payment is confirmed, this qualifies for immediate refund OR order recreation per customer preference
4. Escalate to the billing team within 24 hours
5. Customer should receive resolution within 48 hours

## Refund Process
1. Customer submits refund request with order ID or transaction reference
2. Support agent verifies eligibility
3. If approved, refund is initiated within 3-5 business days
4. Customer receives email confirmation of refund initiation
5. Funds appear in customer account within 5-10 business days depending on payment method

## Non-Refundable Items
- Transaction fees charged by payment processors
- Subscription fees for periods already consumed
- Customized or personalized products once production has begun

## Escalation
Refund requests exceeding $500 or involving suspected fraud must be escalated to the billing manager.
